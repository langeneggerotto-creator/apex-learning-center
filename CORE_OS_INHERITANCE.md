## Repository-specific responsibility

The Learning Center and Fishing School must convert readiness gaps into guided capability growth without removing the learner from the process.

It must support:

1. awareness of purpose, need, route, trade-offs, and risks;
2. desire and appetite assessment without manipulation;
3. knowledge acquisition through books, research, examples, mentors, and instruction;
4. practice that produces demonstrated ability;
5. accommodations, support, and route adaptation when capability or capacity differs;
6. reinforcement, feedback, accountability, maintenance, and continued learning;
7. method visibility: what was searched, why, what was found, what remains unknown, and how decisions were made;
8. teach-back and independent repetition;
9. evidence that learning changed performance, not merely confidence or completion.

## Truth boundary

Reading, viewing, course completion, or self-reported understanding does not prove ability. Operational compliance requires observed or otherwise evidenced performance, appropriate support, and sustained use.
